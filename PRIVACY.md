# Privacy Policy for Hilal'ify: Azan Times

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Hilal'ify: Azan Times ("the app") shows prayer times, sends prayer notifications, and provides home-screen widgets. This policy explains what data the app uses.

## Data we collect

- **Location (optional):** The app uses your approximate or precise location to determine prayer times for where you are. Your location is processed on your device. You can instead choose a city manually.
- **Settings:** Your preferences (language, theme, calculation method, notifications, selected city) are stored only on your device.

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Data sent to third parties

To provide prayer times and city search, the app sends requests to:
- **Aladhan API** (api.aladhan.com): sends coordinates or city and date to get prayer times.
- **Open-Meteo Geocoding** (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com): sends a city name when you search for a city.
- **Diyanet** (namazvakitleri.diyanet.gov.tr): used to read the Hijri date.

These services receive only the information needed for each request. Their own privacy policies apply to that data.

## Permissions

- **Location:** to calculate prayer times for your area.
- **Notifications:** to show prayer reminders.
- **Exact alarms:** to notify you at prayer times.
- **Boot completed:** to restore reminders after the device restarts.
- **Internet:** to fetch prayer times.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children.
